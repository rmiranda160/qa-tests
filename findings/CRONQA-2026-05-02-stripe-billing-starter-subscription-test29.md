## Findings

### ✅ F1 — Stripe Checkout Integration Works End-to-End
The full payment flow from user registration through Stripe Checkout to subscription activation works correctly.
- **Expected:** User can subscribe to Starter (39€/month) via Stripe Checkout
- **Actual:** Subscription activated, success page shown, subscription dashboard reflects "Starter Activa"
- **Status:** PASS ✅

### ⚠️ F2 — Billing Page Does Not Show Invoice Immediately
After successful payment, the billing/invoices page (`/es/module/zonacncplans/billing`) shows "Sin movimientos todavía" (no transactions yet).
- **Expected:** Invoice should appear in billing history immediately after successful charge
- **Actual:** Empty billing history despite confirmed subscription
- **Possible causes:**
  - Stripe webhook not yet processed (async delay)
  - Missing webhook endpoint to sync invoices from Stripe to PrestaShop
  - Billing module only shows next-cycle invoices, not the initial one
- **Status:** ⚠️ WARNING — needs investigation

### ⚠️ F3 — Card Accordion Requires Programmatic Click
The Stripe Checkout "Pay with card" accordion button is not natively clickable via Playwright's `locator.click()` — it reports as "not visible".
- **Expected:** User can click the accordion toggle to expand card fields
- **Actual:** Required `page.evaluate()` to dispatch a `MouseEvent('click')` on the accordion header
- **Status:** ⚠️ WARNING — UX issue or Stripe overlay interference

### ✅ F4 — Billing Address Required Before Checkout
When navigating to pricing without a billing address, a warning was shown requiring address completion first.
- **Status:** PASS ✅ (address form works correctly)
